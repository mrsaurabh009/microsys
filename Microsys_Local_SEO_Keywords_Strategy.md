## SECTION 05 — Hard-Code SEO Checklist
### Ye sab hardcode karna mandatory hai — ranking guarantee ke liye

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT:** Ye section sabse critical hai. Keywords dhundh lene se ranking nahi aati — inhein sahi jagah place karna padta hai. Ye ek ek item implement karo.

---

### 1. META TAGS — Har Page Pe Mandatory

**Homepage Meta Tags:**

```html
<title>Microsys — Laptop, Desktop & Gaming PC Shop in Biharsharif, Nalanda Bihar</title>

<meta name="description" content="Microsys is Biharsharif's leading computer showroom. Buy laptops, gaming PCs, desktops, printers & PC components at the best price. Visit us at Ramchandarpur Bus Stand, Nalanda, Bihar.">

<meta name="keywords" content="laptop biharsharif, computer store nalanda, gaming pc bihar, printer shop biharsharif, desktop pc nalanda">
```

| Rule | Specification |
|------|--------------|
| Title Length | 50-60 characters max — include primary keyword + city |
| Description Length | 150-160 characters max — include city + call to action |
| Unique Per Page | Every page must have different title and description |
| Keyword In Title | Primary keyword must appear in first 60 chars of title |
| Brand In Title | Include "Microsys" in title of all important pages |

---

### 2. HEADING STRUCTURE — H1 to H3

| Tag | Rule | Example |
|-----|------|---------|

| H3 | Subsection — product names, features | HP 15s Core i3 — Best Budget Laptop in Bihar |
