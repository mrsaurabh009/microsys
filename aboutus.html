About Microsys — Biharsharif's Most Complete Computer Store

Serving Bihar Sharif and Nalanda with genuine hardware, expert advice, and trusted after-sales support since 2015.

Microsys is Bihar Sharif’s most trusted and largest computer retail showroom, located at Ramchandarpur Bus Stand, Nalanda, Bihar. For technology buyers across Biharsharif, Nalanda, Rajgir, Islampur, and the surrounding districts, Microsys has established itself as the definitive destination for all computing needs — under one roof.

We stock and sell a comprehensive range of technology products including laptops, desktop computers, gaming PCs, custom-built PC systems, PC components, tablets, printers, motherboards, RAM, storage drives, keyboards, and a wide selection of computer peripherals. Whether you are a student buying your first laptop, a business owner setting up a workstation, or a gamer building a high-performance rig — Microsys has the right solution at the right price.

At Microsys, we believe that quality technology should be accessible to every household and business in Bihar. That is why we offer genuine products from trusted global brands, transparent pricing, and honest after-sales guidance — no hidden costs, no compromises.

Our showroom in Biharsharif is designed to give you a hands-on experience before you buy. Walk in, compare products, get expert advice from our trained staff, and walk out with the perfect device — confidently.
