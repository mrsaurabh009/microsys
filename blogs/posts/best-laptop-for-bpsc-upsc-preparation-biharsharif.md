## Battery Life Matters: Studying Through Power Outages and Long Library Sessions

Many students in Biharsharif study in self-study libraries around Ramchandarpur, Khandak Par, and Hospital Road, or experience occasional power fluctuations during peak summer months. 

- **Battery Capacity:** Look for a minimum of a **3-cell 41Wh to 50Wh battery** capable of delivering at least 6 to 8 hours of continuous video playback or PDF reading on a single charge.
- **Fast Charging:** Features like HP Fast Charge or Lenovo Rapid Charge allow you to replenish 50% of the battery in just 45 minutes, ensuring you never miss a morning test session.
- **Type-C Charging:** Newer models supporting USB-C Power Delivery let you charge with compact adapters or compatible high-capacity power banks.

## Offline Study Support: SSD Storage for Massive PDF Libraries and Video Lectures

Never purchase a laptop with an old-school mechanical hard disk drive (HDD) in 2026. A mechanical drive takes minutes to boot and slows down every time you open a large folder.

- **Fast NVMe SSD Storage:** Insist on a minimum of **512GB PCIe NVMe SSD**. An SSD boots Windows 11 in under 10 seconds and instantly searches through thousands of scanned PDFs.
- **Offline Download Archives:** Civil services preparation requires downloading dozens of gigabytes of monthly magazine archives, test papers, and offline video lectures. 512GB ensures ample room without needing external hard drives immediately.
- **8GB to 16GB RAM:** Ensure the laptop has at least **8GB DDR4/DDR5 RAM**, preferably with an open slot for an affordable upgrade to 16GB later.

## Eye Comfort: Anti-Glare Displays for 8+ Hour Study Marathons

Aspirants spend 8 to 12 hours every day staring at screens. Screen quality directly impacts your eyesight and headaches:

- **FHD Resolution (1920x1080):** Avoid low-resolution 720p displays; Full HD ensures razor-sharp text clarity when reading fine-print PDFs.
- **Anti-Glare Coating (Matte Screen):** A matte screen diffuses ambient room lighting and sunlight, preventing annoying reflections and reducing eye strain.
- **TÜV Rheinland Low Blue Light Certification:** Minimizes blue light emissions so you can study late into the night without disrupting your sleep cycle.
