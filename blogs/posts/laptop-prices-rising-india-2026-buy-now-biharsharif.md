## Why Are Prices Rising?

Laptop price inflation in 2026 is not an isolated phenomenon; it is driven by a convergence of global supply chain shifts, semiconductor production costs, and domestic trade policies:

1. **Global Semiconductor & Memory Price Spikes:**
   Key semiconductor foundries and memory manufacturers (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron) have shifted production capacity toward high-margin enterprise AI server chips. This has tightened the supply of standard consumer DDR5 RAM, LPDDR5X chips, and PCIe Gen 4 NAND flash, pushing basic component costs up by 15% to 25% worldwide.
   
2. **Stricter Import Compliance & Tariffs:**
   The Government of India has instituted tightened import management protocols and revised customs valuation guidelines on fully assembled electronic devices to bolster domestic manufacturing under the "Make in India" initiative. While positive for long-term domestic assembly, transitional licensing overheads have increased short-term landing costs for imported consumer electronics.

3. **Currency Fluctuation and Freight Logistics:**
   Fluctuations in the USD-INR exchange rate combined with elevated global air and sea shipping tariffs have compounded wholesale landing costs for major brands including HP, Dell, Lenovo, and ASUS.

4. **Integration of On-Device AI Hardware:**
   Modern 2026 processors (Intel Core Ultra, AMD Ryzen 8000/9000, and Qualcomm Snapdragon X) now mandate integrated Neural Processing Units (NPUs) for Windows 11 Copilot features, pushing base manufacturing bill-of-materials higher across entry and midrange tiers.
