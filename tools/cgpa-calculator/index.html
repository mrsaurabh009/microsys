What is the Percentage to CGPA Formula for Indian Students?

Converting your percentage to CGPA is essential when applying to universities abroad or filling academic forms. Most Indian universities follow the CBSE standard formula for converting percentage to CGPA on a 10-point scale. This tool is trusted by students across India applying to the USA, UK, Germany, Canada, Australia, Ireland, and Singapore.

Standard Formula — Percentage to CGPA (10-Point Scale):
CGPA = Percentage / 9.5   (Example: 80% / 9.5 = CGPA 8.42 out of 10)

Frequently Asked Questions (CGPA & Percentage Conversion)

How is CGPA converted to percentage on a 10-point scale?

For Indian secondary boards and universities adhering to CBSE and AICTE guidelines, CGPA is multiplied by 9.5: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. For example, a CGPA of 8.0 corresponds to 8.0 × 9.5 = 76.0%. To reverse the calculation, divide percentage by 9.5.

What is the conversion formula for a 7-point CGPA scale?

On a 7-point grading scale (common in state universities such as Mumbai University), the standard formula is: Percentage = (CGPA / 7) × 100. For instance, a 5.6 CGPA on a 7-point scale equals (5.6 / 7) × 100 = 80.0%.

Why is CGPA converted to a 4.0 GPA scale for study abroad?

Universities in the USA, Canada, Germany, and the UK evaluate international academic credentials using the 4.0 GPA scale for admissions and scholarship decisions. A percentage of 80% or above generally correlates to a competitive 3.7 to 4.0 US GPA range, qualifying candidates for direct Master's and PhD admissions.
