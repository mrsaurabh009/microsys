Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How is the laptop loan EMI calculated?

The monthly installment is calculated using the standard reducing balance mathematical formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1], where P represents the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and 100), and N is the tenure expressed in months.

What documents are required for showroom laptop financing?

Typically, you need government-issued identity proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card), active bank account details (or debit/credit card), and a mobile phone linked to Aadhaar for instant biometric/OTP verification.
